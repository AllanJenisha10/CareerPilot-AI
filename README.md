## 📌 About the Project

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students, fresh graduates, and job seekers make informed career decisions. By leveraging Google's Gemini AI, the platform provides a foundation for personalized career recommendations, skill assessments, skill-gap analysis, learning roadmaps, and resume improvement guidance.

CareerPilot AI aims to simplify career planning by bringing career exploration, skill development, and job readiness tools together in one user-friendly application.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Make career guidance more accessible and personalized.
- Help users explore career paths aligned with their interests and goals.
- Identify skill gaps and encourage continuous learning.
- Support resume preparation and professional development.
- Provide a centralized platform for career exploration and planning.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎯 **Career Recommendations** — Explore career paths based on your interests, skills, and goals.
- 🧠 **Skill Assessment** — Understand your current skills and areas for improvement.
- 📊 **Skill-Gap Analysis** — Identify skills that may be needed for your desired career.
- 🗺️ **Learning Roadmaps** — Access structured suggestions for learning and career development.
- 📄 **Resume Assistance** — Get guidance on improving resume content and presentation.
- 🤖 **Gemini AI Integration** — Generate AI-powered career guidance.
- 💻 **User-Friendly Interface** — Navigate career-planning features through a consistent interface.
- 📱 **Responsive Experience** — Use the platform on supported screen sizes.
- 🛡️ **Error Handling** — Display helpful feedback when AI services are temporarily unavailable.

Feature availability depends on the current implementation of the application.
